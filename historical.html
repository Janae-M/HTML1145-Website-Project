The Origins of Music

Music has accompanied human communities dating all the way back to ancient times. People used singing, percussion, and other instruments for ceremonies, storytelling, celebrations, and social connection.

Musical characteristics

Rhythn, repetition, chanting, and music passed down through oral traditions.

Instruments and sounds

Drums, rattles, flutes, and other instruments made from materials available in local communities.

People and traditions to explore

Ancient Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Greek, and other musical traditions around the world.
