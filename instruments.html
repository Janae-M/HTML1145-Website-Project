Meet the families

Explore Instrument Families

Different instruments create sound in different ways.

🎻

String Instruments

Strings vibrate when plucked, bowed, or struck. Examples include the violin, guitar, cello, harp, and double bass.

🪈

Woodwind Instruments

Players direct air through or across an instrument. Examples include the flute, clarinet, oboe, and bassoon.

🎺

Brass Instruments

Players buzz their lips into a mouthpiece. The trumpet, trombone, French horn, and tuba belong to this family.

🥁

Percussion Instruments

Sound comes from striking, shaking, or scraping. Examples include drums, cymbals, tambourines, and xylophones.

🎹

Keyboards

Keyboard instruments include the piano, organ, and synthesizer, each producing sound through a different mechanism.

🎛️

Electronic Instruments

Synthesizers, electronic drum machines, and digital controllers help musicians create and shape electronic sounds.

Listen closely

What makes every instrument unique?

Pitch, tone, volume, and playing technique all help shape an instrument's sound. Try listening to the same melody played on a piano, guitar, and violin.
